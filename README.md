What I’m Trying to Understand

One question keeps coming up as I study:

How does an organization know who someone is, what they’re allowed to access, and when that access should change or disappear?

That question is at the center of IAM.

For example, imagine a new employee joins a company.

They need an identity.

That identity needs to be authenticated.

Their identity needs to be authorized for specific resources.

Their access needs to be controlled.

And when their role changes or they leave the organization, their access needs to change or be removed.

That’s the part of IT security I’m becoming increasingly interested in.

SC-900 Topics I’m Studying

Identity

Understanding identities, identity providers, directory services, and how identity fits into an organization’s security model.

Authentication

Understanding how a system verifies that someone or something is who it claims to be.

Authorization

Understanding how systems determine what an authenticated identity is actually allowed to do.

Microsoft Entra ID

Learning the fundamentals of Microsoft’s cloud-based identity and access management platform and how organizations use it to manage identities and access.

Zero Trust

Learning why modern security models operate around principles such as:

* Verify explicitly
* Use least privilege
* Assume breach

The biggest thing I’m taking away so far is that IAM isn’t simply about creating accounts.
